ShiftLog

ShiftLog is a shift tracking and savings tool I built while working through school. I was calculating my hours and pay in my head or on a calculator, so I built something to do it for me. It logs my shifts, calculates gross pay (including night premium hours), records my real paychecks and expenses, and tracks my savings against a goal.
